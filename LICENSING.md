# Licensing

Statedash is released under a **dual-licensing** model.

## 1. Open source — AGPL-3.0

The default licence is the [GNU Affero General Public License v3.0](LICENSE).

You may freely use, study, modify and self-host Statedash, including inside a
company, at no cost. In exchange the AGPL asks for one thing: **anything you
build on top of it stays open under the same licence.**

Concretely, if you distribute a modified Statedash — or let anyone else use it
over a network, which is the usual case for a web dashboard — you must offer
those users the complete corresponding source code of your version, under the
AGPL. Section 13 of the licence closes the "we only run it as a service, we
never ship it" loophole that ordinary GPL leaves open.

Running Statedash unmodified on your own firewall for your own needs triggers
none of this.

## 2. Commercial licence

The AGPL is deliberately incompatible with shipping Statedash as part of a
closed-source or permissively licensed product. If that is what you need, a
separate commercial licence is available on negotiated terms.

You need one if you want to:

- bundle Statedash into a product distributed under a licence other than the
  AGPL — a BSD-2-Clause firewall distribution, for instance;
- offer it as part of a hosted or managed service without publishing your
  modifications;
- integrate parts of the code into proprietary software.

Contact: **exandore@gmail.com**

The copyright holder is the only party who can grant this, which is why
contributions are handled the way described below.

## 3. Contributions

Merge requests are welcome and the project is meant to be developed in the
open. Because the commercial licence above must remain grantable, every
contributor is asked to sign the Contributor Licence Agreement described in
[CONTRIBUTING.md](CONTRIBUTING.md) — it lets the copyright holder relicense
contributed code, while you keep full ownership of what you wrote.

Without it a single contributed patch would make the commercial licence
impossible to issue for the whole project.
